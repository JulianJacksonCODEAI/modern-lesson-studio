LESSON STUDIO {VERSION}: HOW TO INSTALL
=======================================

Lesson Studio copies a lesson's Google Slides speaker notes and Notion page into
a Level Builder lesson. It runs in Google Chrome, in the side panel.

INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
1. Unzip this file. You get this guide and a folder named "lesson-studio".
   Move the "lesson-studio" folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents.
   Chrome loads it from there, so don't delete or move it later.
2. In Chrome, go to:  chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" (top left) and choose the "lesson-studio" folder.
5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin "Lesson Studio".
6. Click the Lesson Studio icon to open the side panel.

Sign in to Chrome with your code.org Google account, and to Level Builder.

USE IT
1. Import: open the lesson's Slides deck and click "Import the open Slides deck".
   Then open its Notion page and click "Import the open Notion page".
2. Check: read what will go into Level Builder. You can't edit here. If something
   is wrong, click "Report" on that item.
3. Save: open YOUR lesson in Level Builder, tick "This is my lesson", click
   "Check resources", choose Skip or Add for anything missing, click
   "Prepare changes", read the list, then tick the box and click
   "Save to Level Builder". Make any edits in Level Builder afterwards.

Only save to lessons you own. Never save to an official course lesson.

SOMETHING WRONG?
Click "Report a problem" at the bottom of the side panel. Your report goes to
the curriculum team with the details of your run filled in.

UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
1. Unzip the new version.
2. Delete everything inside your existing "lesson-studio" folder, then copy in
   everything from the new "lesson-studio" folder. Keep the folder where it is.
3. Go to chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow on Lesson Studio.
This keeps your imported lesson and any reports still waiting to send.
The panel's footer shows the version you're using.
