*Last updated November 23, 2026 (version 1.6.0)*

Lesson Studio is a browser extension used by the Code.org CodeAI curriculum team. It copies a lesson's Google Slides speaker notes and Notion page into a Code.org Level Builder lesson.

## What Lesson Studio reads

- **The Google Slides deck you import**, when you click "Import the open Slides deck". It reads the slides and speaker notes. Nothing in Slides is changed.
- **The Notion page you import**, when you click "Import the open Notion page". Nothing in Notion is changed.
- **The Level Builder lesson you choose**, after you confirm it is your lesson. Lesson Studio reads it, saves the changes you approve, then reads it back to check the save.

It reads no other pages, and it runs only when you use it.

## What is stored, and for how long

The imported lesson, save records and any unsent problem reports are kept in the browser's memory for the browser session. They are deleted when you close the browser, choose "Start over" or send the report. Nothing from a lesson is written to disk.

Settings (your AI choice) are kept in the browser until you remove the extension.

## What is sent, and to whom

- **Level Builder** receives the lesson changes you approve, saved to the lesson you chose.
- **The CodeAI team's Google Form** receives a problem report only when you choose "Report a problem" and send it. A report includes the lesson title and Level Builder link, the Slides and Notion links, the item you flagged, the save result and your note. Google Forms records your code.org email address with the report so the team can follow up. Reports are read only by the CodeAI curriculum team.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. AI suggestions use Chrome's built-in model, which runs on your computer, so lesson text is not sent to any AI service. Lesson Studio has no analytics, tracking or advertising, and no data is sold or shared with anyone else.

## Your choices

- Choose "Start over" in the side panel, or close the browser, to delete the imported lesson.
- Don't send a problem report if you don't want your email recorded. You can still use every other feature.
- Remove the extension to delete its settings.
